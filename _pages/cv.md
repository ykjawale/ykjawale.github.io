## Research Experience

### **IISER Pune, IN**

* **Open Source Image Analysis:** Built quantitative flow-tracking software for intracellular microscopy by leveraging algorithms historically used in observational big-data astronomy.

**Master’s Student** (Chaitanya Athale Group) – *Jun. 2017 – Apr. 2018*
* **Microtubule Transport Instability:** Investigated the physical role of external forcing on the directional transport dynamics of microtubule-motor networks.

**Semester & Summer Projects** – *May 2015 – Apr. 2017*
* **Gliding Assays:** Performed *in vitro* reconstitution and physical quantification of recombinant Kinesin-1 (KHC) gliding assays.
* **Foldscope Optimization:** Optimized low-cost cell phone-based microscopy using custom PDMS stages, glass lenses, and 3D-printed focus mechanisms.
* **iGEM Team Member** (2015)**:** Contributed to "Mycobacterium Revelio," <a href="http://2015.igem.org/Team:IISER_Pune" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><i class="fas fa-bacterium"></i></a> developing genetic circuit components and characterization assays (Awarded Bronze Medal <i class="fas fa-medal"></i> at Giant Jamboree).
