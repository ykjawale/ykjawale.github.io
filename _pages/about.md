I am a biophysicist and quantitative cell biologist. I currently work as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Marileen Dogterom Group at TU Delft, where I also completed my Ph.D. in September 2025.

My current research sits at the interface of bottom-up synthetic biology and cytoskeleton. I aim to understand the quantitative physical principles that drive active cellular processes, such as asymmetric spindle positioning and cell division, by reconstituting minimal active cytoskeletal networks inside cell-like confinement.

## Research & Core Expertise

To address key questions in quantitative biology, I employ a hybrid "wet + dry" research approach:
* **Biophysical Reconstitutions:** Bottom-up assembly of cytoskeletal networks (microtubules, motor proteins) and GUV encapsulation using cDICE.
* **Experimental Control & Manipulation**: Spatial and temporal control of cellular and cytoskeletal processes using optogenetic tools and microfluidic approaches to manipulate cell shape, geometry, and mechanical confinement.
* **Advanced Quantitative Microscopy:** Spinning disc confocal, TIRF, and IRM imaging to resolve fast, sub-cellular dynamics.
* **Image & Data Analysis:** Quantitative image processing and analysis using Fiji/ImageJ, together with custom workflows for segmentation, tracking, feature extraction, and measurement of dynamics.
* **Physical Modeling & Computations:** Utilizing numerical simulations (Cytosim, Molecular Dynamics) and custom data pipelines (Python, MATLAB, R) to quantitatively decode and model complex biological systems.
